# Pharmazz, Inc.

> Clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in Willowbrook, Illinois, developing first-in-class
> therapeutics for critical care and neurovascular medicine. Pharmazz publishes no public API,
> SDK, developer portal or machine-readable specification of any kind.

Pharmazz, Inc. is a privately held Delaware corporation founded and led by pharmacologist
Dr. Anil Gulati. Its two lead small-molecule candidates are centhaquine (Lyfaquin), a
resuscitative agent for hypovolemic shock that holds marketing authorization from the Drugs
Controller General of India and is licensed to Dr. Reddy's Laboratories for the Indian market,
and sovateltide, an endothelin-B receptor agonist for cerebral ischemic stroke. Additional
pipeline programs target septic shock, acute kidney injury, cardiac arrest, acute spinal cord
injury, hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy and Alzheimer's disease.
